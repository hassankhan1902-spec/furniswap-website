Common questions

What condition does my old furniture need to be in?

We accept furniture in almost any usable condition — from like-new items to well-used family furniture. The condition decides how much discount you get on your new order. Even older sofas or bed frames still have value!

Do I have to pay anything for a price check?

No, the estimate is 100% free with zero obligation. You can send photos on WhatsApp or ask for a free home visit. If you don't like our offer, you don't have to pay a single rupee.

Can I swap just one piece (like only a sofa or only a bed)?

Yes, of course! You can swap a single sofa set, just a bed, a dining table, or multiple rooms together. You are never forced to buy a full set.

How do I pay?

You only pay the remaining difference after your old furniture discount is deducted. You can pay via direct bank transfer, credit/debit card, or cash on delivery.

Which areas in Islamabad and Rawalpindi do you deliver to?

We cover all residential areas including DHA (Phases 1, 2, 5), Bahria Town (Phases 1–8), Gulberg Greens, Chaklala, and all Islamabad sectors (F-6, F-7, F-8, F-10, F-11, G-11, I-8, E-7, and surrounding areas).
