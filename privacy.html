Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

FurniSwap ("we", "us") helps families in Islamabad and Rawalpindi trade in old furniture for credit towards new pieces. This page explains what information we collect through furniswap.pk, why we collect it, and how we look after it.

What we collect

  • Photo quote form: your name, your WhatsApp number, and the photos of furniture you upload. Photos are resized in your browser before upload.
  • Messages you send us on WhatsApp, including any photos or details you share there.
  • Basic technical data that any website receives, such as your IP address and browser type. Our hosting provider uses this for security and spam protection.

We do not ask for payment card details on this website.

How we use it

  • To review your furniture and prepare a trade-in quote.
  • To contact you on WhatsApp about your quote, pickup and delivery.
  • To compare your furniture with similar items for sale, so our price is fair. We may use automated tools, including AI services, to help with this. A member of our team reviews every quote before it is sent to you.
  • To protect the website from spam and abuse.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Where it is stored

Photos and form details are stored in FurniSwap's own Google Drive. The website is hosted by Cloudflare. These providers process the data on our behalf under their own security and privacy terms. If we use an AI service to help estimate prices, it receives only the photos and item details it needs.

Google user data

Our website connects to Google Drive only to save quote submissions into FurniSwap's own Drive account. It uses the limited drive.file permission, which only allows access to files the website itself creates. We never ask visitors to sign in with Google, and we do not access visitors' Google accounts. Our use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

How long we keep it

We keep quote photos and details for as long as we need them to handle your quote and any swap that follows, and for our records after that. You can ask us to delete them at any time.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the information we hold about you. Send us a WhatsApp message with your quote reference number (for example FS-20261125-7K3Q) and we will take care of it.
